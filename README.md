# Hi, I'm Michael 👋

I spent nine years writing about technology as a journalist — now I build the data infrastructure a newsroom runs on.
As **Head of Data & AI at [KURIER](https://kurier.at)** in Vienna, I sit somewhere between the editorial desk and the data warehouse, and I like it there.

## 🛠️ What I'm working on

- Our **tracking setup** — making it reliable, understandable and easier to maintain
- Our **data lake** on Google Cloud / BigQuery, modelled with Dataform
- **Performance data** for the newsroom and **data journalism** projects

## 💡 What I'm interested in

- **Data journalism tools** — visualization, crunching large datasets, geodata
- **Open data**, especially in Austria — and what the new Freedom of Information Act (*Informationsfreiheitsgesetz*) makes possible
- **Tools for journalists** that take the friction out of everyday newsroom work
- **Bookmarklets** — small, sharp tools that live right in the browser

If you're working on something in that space, I'd love to hear about it.

## 📚 Background

- **9 years** as a tech journalist at [futurezone.at](https://futurezone.at)
- At **KURIER since 2019**, working with data in different roles — **Head of Data & AI since 2025**
- Part of the **first cohort of [42 Vienna](https://42vienna.com)**, completed the Common Core in 2025 (C, C++, Python, backend development with Django)
